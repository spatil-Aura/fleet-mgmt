Fleet Renewal prototype
  1. Manage the fleet to open Fleet Details. Hover a Needed tag, try Edit Number and Remove.
  2. Click Calculate Fleet Renewal Fees (Renew Now on the site). Like the site, it opens Calculating Fleet Renewal Fees (on the site, a notification arrives once the fees are ready). Click Back. In this prototype the fees are now ready, so Fleet Details adds Total Fees next to Vehicles Being Renewed and the button changes to Review Fees.
  3. Click Review Fees. The balance is short, so the Insufficient Funds popup opens.
  4. In that popup, click Change Account and pick Fleet Operations. Review Fees now goes to Fees Overview, then Pay Now to the confirmation.
  5. After renewing, open the Registration menu at the top, click Fleet Management, then Manage. The fleet shows Renewing while it runs. Click to skip ahead: the fleet becomes Active and Registrations That Need Attention appears (like the site, only once the renewal is complete). Open it and click Details on a vehicle.
  6. Try Remove Fleet at the bottom of Fleet Details. Once a fleet is Renewing, Review Fees and Remove Fleet are grayed out, so click Restart first if you’ve already renewed.
  7. To see Change Account grayed out, tick , then click Review Fees.
